Unit 1

Atomic Structure

Atoms are the fundamental building blocks of all chemical matter. This unit explores subatomic constituents, electromagnetic radiation, quantum mechanical models, electron orbital distributions, and periodic properties.

Topic 1

Discovery & Atomic Basics

Discovery of subatomic particles, atomic models, isotopes, relative masses, and atomic notation.

Topic 2

Waves, Spectra & Orbitals

Electromagnetic spectrum, Planck's relation, Bohr model & hydrogen line spectra, quantum numbers ($n, l, m_l, m_s$), and orbital geometries.

Topic 3

Electronic Configuration

Aufbau principle, Pauli exclusion principle, Hund's rule, noble gas notation, and transition metal exceptions.

Topic 4

Periodic Table & Trends

Periodic law, atomic/ionic radius, first and successive ionization energies, electron affinity, and electronegativity trends.
