Unit 3

Chemical Calculations

Stoichiometry provides the quantitative mathematical framework of chemistry. This unit covers the mole concept, empirical and molecular formulae, balanced equation calculations, limiting reactants, solution concentrations, volumetric analysis, and reaction yields.

Topic 1

The Mole & Avogadro Constant

Avogadro constant ($L = 6.022 \times 10^{23}\text{ mol}^{-1}$), molar mass ($M$), and particle count conversions.

Topic 2

Empirical & Molecular Formulas

Percentage composition by mass, combustion analysis, determining empirical formula, and molecular formula determination.

Topic 3

Stoichiometry & Limiting Reactants

Mole-to-mole and mass-to-mass stoichiometry, identifying limiting and excess reactants, theoretical and percentage yields.

Topic 4

Solution Concentrations

Molarity ($c = n/V$), mass concentration ($\text{g dm}^{-3}$), parts per million ($\text{ppm}$), and serial dilutions ($c_1V_1 = c_2V_2$).

Topic 5

Volumetric Titrations

Acid-base titrations, redox titrations (permanganometry, iodometry), primary standards, equivalence points, and indicators.

Topic 6

Gas Calculations & Yields

Molar gas volume ($22.7\text{ dm}^3\text{ mol}^{-1}$ at STP / $24.0\text{ dm}^3\text{ mol}^{-1}$ at RTP), ideal gas equation $PV=nRT$, and atom economy.
