Why Graphite Rods?

  • Chemically Inert: Unreactive with HCl or salts. Zero contamination.
  • High Melting Point: Withstands Bunsen flame heat (~3600°C) without melting.

Physics of Emission

How it works

Heat energy is absorbed, causing an electron to jump to a higher Excited State. As it relaxes back to the Ground State, it releases the excess energy as a visible photon (color).
