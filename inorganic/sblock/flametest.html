Flame Tests

Flame Test Laboratory

Drag the graphite rod to a salt sample to coat it, then move it into the flame to observe the emission spectrum. Use HCl to clean the rod.

Why Graphite Rods?

  • Chemically Inert: Graphite does not react with the Hydrochloric acid (HCl) or the salt samples, ensuring no contamination of the results.
  • High Melting Point: Carbon (Graphite) has an extremely high melting point (~3600°C), so it withstands the Bunsen flame without melting.

Physics of Emission

The heat energy from the flame excites electrons to higher, unstable energy levels. As these electrons relax back to their ground state, they emit the absorbed energy as photons of specific visible wavelengths.

$\Delta E = h\nu = \frac{hc}{\lambda}$
