s-Block Elements

The s-block consists of Group 1 (Alkali Metals) and Group 2 (Alkaline Earth Metals). Their chemistry is dominated by the ease with which they lose their valence s-electrons to form cations.

Diagonal Relationship

"Some elements of the second period behave like the elements in the third period placed diagonally to each other."

Important Note: This similarity arises because these pairs have similar ionic potential (charge/size ratio). For example, BeO and Al₂O₃ are both amphoteric.
