Alkanes

Alkanes are saturated hydrocarbons containing only single bonds ($C-C$ and $C-H$).

General Formula $C_nH_{2n+2}$
3.1 Structure

Homologous Series

Methane ($CH_4$)

Ethane ($C_2H_6$)

3.2 Reactions

Free Radical Substitution

$CH_4 + Cl_2 \xrightarrow{UV} CH_3Cl + HCl$

Mechanism Steps:

  1. Initiation: $Cl-Cl \to 2Cl^{\bullet}$
  2. Propagation: $CH_4 + Cl^{\bullet} \to CH_3^{\bullet} + HCl$
  3. Termination: $Cl^{\bullet} + Cl^{\bullet} \to Cl_2$
