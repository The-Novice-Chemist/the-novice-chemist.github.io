Chemical Equilibria

Chemical Equilibria

In a reversible reaction, equilibrium is reached when the rates of forward and backward reactions are equal.

Le Chatelier's Principle

If a change is made to a system at equilibrium, the position of equilibrium moves to counteract the change.
