Activation Energy

3.1 Activation Energy

Activation energy is the energy difference between the reactants and the transition state.

$$ E_a = E_{\text{transition state}} - E_{\text{reactants}} $$

Energy Profile Diagram

Important Point

Rate is indirectly proportional to activation energy.

  • Higher activation energy → Slower reaction
  • Lower activation energy → Faster reaction

3.2 Elementary Reactions

Elementary reactions are reactions that occur in a single step and cannot be simplified further.

Two types of mechanisms:

Simple Mechanism

Occurs in one elementary step.

Complex Mechanism

Occurs in multiple elementary steps.
