The Concept

Concept

In the study of any chemical reaction, we try to find out the following two things:

1.1 Feasibility of a Reaction

This determines whether a chemical reaction is energetically possible under given conditions.

$\Delta G < 0$ → Thermodynamically favorable (Feasible)
$\Delta G > 0$ → Thermodynamically unfavorable (Not Feasible)

* $\Delta G$ represents Gibbs Free Energy

Feasible ($\Delta G < 0$)

Not Feasible ($\Delta G > 0$)

This tells us how far a reaction proceeds.

1.3 Thermodynamic and Kinetic Requirements

For a reaction to occur, it must satisfy two conditions:

Thermodynamically Favored

"Can it happen?"

  • The reaction must be energetically feasible.
  • It must be able to form the desired amount of products.

Kinetically Favored

"How fast does it happen?"

The reaction must occur within the desired time.

Depends on:

Rate of reaction Temperature Activation energy

1.4 Ionic Reactions

Ionic reactions are relatively fast.

This is because bonds do not need to be broken.
